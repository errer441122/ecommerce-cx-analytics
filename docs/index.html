Handle the late order before it becomes a bad review.

On 95,000 real reviews, 83% of on-time orders were rated 4-5 stars and only 27% of late ones. These two tools turn that into a daily routine on your own exports. Everything runs in this page: your files never leave your computer.

Loading the analysis engine (about 10 seconds the first time)

Late orders, every morning

From the export of your shipping platform or carrier: the orders past the promised date and not delivered yet, longest wait first, to contact before the customer complains and to keep out of promotions in Klaviyo (delivery_issue=open); the ones delivered late this week, for a follow-up; and the late rate by carrier.

Your column names

Negative reviews, by urgency and owner

From any reviews export with a rating and the text: reviews at or below the rating you choose, most urgent first (not received, then product problems, then delivery and service), each with the team it goes to, plus how often each theme comes up. The themes are keyword lists you can read, not a language model.

Your column names (common ones are recognised)

The page runs the repository's own Python code in your browser with Pyodide: the same functions and tests as the command line. Example files are handwritten with example.com addresses; the Olist reviews are real public data (Kaggle, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).
