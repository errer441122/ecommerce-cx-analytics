# Two years of credit notes at a real wholesaler

805,828 invoice and cancellation lines, 5,852 customers (UCI *Online Retail II*, a UK gift wholesaler, December 2009 to December 2011). 2,444 customers have at least one credit line. My own RFM analysis of this data drops those lines in the first cleaning step.

## What the credit lines are

| Kind | Lines | Value |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Goods sent back, at least a day after the purchase | 13,536 | GBP 265,566 |
| Same-day corrections | 2,505 | GBP 406,764 |
| No earlier purchase in the file | 1,600 | GBP 40,680 |

57% of the credited money was cancelled on the day it was invoiced.

## Is a customer who sends goods back leaving?

No. In 10 of 10 groups of similar customers (same number of orders so far, same days since the last one), those who had just sent goods back ordered again at least as often: 79% within 90 days, against 75% (1,995 cases). So the tool points at items, not at customers to save.
